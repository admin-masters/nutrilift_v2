How to screen students

A consistent check-in helps every child receive the right follow-up.

Open PDF guide ↗{% if config.guide_video_url %}Watch screening video ↗{% else %}The programme screening video has not been configured.{% endif %}
01

Prepare & identify

Use the correct school, academic year, class, section and roll number. Never enter a student’s name.

Prepare a digital scale, stadiometer and MUAC tape. Measure without shoes or heavy jackets.

02

Measure & ask

Enter weight in kilograms and height in centimetres. Record MUAC tape colour for children under 60 months; BMI-for-age is calculated for older children.

Answer each health, diet, programme support and food-security question. Ask sensitive questions discreetly.

03

Review & follow up

Red or yellow: notify the parent and re-screen in three calendar months. Green: re-screen in six calendar months.

WhatsApp opening is recorded once. Delivery and reading are unverified. Parent contact details are cleared after use.

04

Keep the history clear

Use Edit responses to correct an earlier answer. Its original date and correction history are retained.

Use Re-screen on or after the due date to save a new dated form with blank screening answers.

Understanding the result

A red finding takes priority over yellow; yellow takes priority over green. The software follows the supplied workbook’s rules, including diet-specific questions and “Yes” to sugary drinks or packaged snacks as a red flag.

BMI uses sex-specific WHO LMS reference data. The WHO reference and cut-offs are documented in the project. A flag identifies a need for follow-up and does not establish a diagnosis.
