Programme terms & privacy

NutriLift supports school growth and nutritional-risk screening. Use it only for school classes you are authorised to manage.

Student records

Records are identified by school, academic year, class, section and roll number. Student names are not collected. Date of birth, sex, measurements and screening answers are stored in NutriLift and visible only to assigned teachers, the school principal, and authorised programme users.

Parent communication

A parent’s name and WhatsApp number are used temporarily on the teacher’s device and are not stored by NutriLift. Choosing Continue to WhatsApp shares the prepared message with WhatsApp. NutriLift records an opening event, not delivery, reading or video viewing.

Programme guidance

Demo records are fictional. Parent messages and videos require programme approval before sharing. A screening flag is not a medical diagnosis.

Record integrity

Corrections retain earlier answers and the person and time responsible for the change. Re-screenings create new dated records.
